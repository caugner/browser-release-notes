Dear Opera Users!

Opera’s latest stable release, version 119, is here!

Introducing a new feature: **Clear Data on Exit**. This option enables you to automatically remove specific data, such as your browsing history or passwords, whenever you close the browser.  Additionally, we’re excited to introduce two new themes **Twilight & Ethereal**!

You can dive into all the details about Clear Data on Exit and themes right here: [Opera One is updated with more dynamic Themes and a new privacy feature](https://blogs.opera.com/desktop/2025/05/opera-one-browser-clear-data-on-exit-privacy-feature/)

Key issues resolved in this update include:

*   The theme management system now works correctly, allowing you to remove the last classic theme effortlessly for complete customization.
*   We’ve improved the Easy Setup feature, ensuring it resumes from where you left off upon reopening, without resetting to the top.
*   Stability has been enhanced to prevent crashes when creating split screens from orphaned and pinned tabs, offering a smoother session.
*   Chromium version bumped to 134.0.6998.205.

For a complete rundown, take a look at the [changelog](http://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-119/).

Happy browsing!
