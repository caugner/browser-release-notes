Dear Opera Users,

We’re happy to announce that Opera 121 has now reached the **stable channel**, based on **Chromium 137.0.7151.122**.

### Key fixes and highlights:

**Password management**

*   Better tooltip support
*   Fixed styling issues in light/dark modes

**Split screen & tab islands**

*   More stable tab island creation and movement
*   Fixed crashes when dragging split screens
*   Improved tooltips and hover states

**Video & calls**

*   Smoother Picture-in-Picture (PiP) behavior
*   Fixed YouTube fullscreen sidebar bug
*   Google Meet stability fixes

**UI & personalization**

*   Updated Messenger icon
*   Clearer toggles, text, and icons in light/dark themes

**VPN Pro & security**

*   New diagnostics section in settings
*   Connection stability improvements
*   Critical Chromium 0-day security patch (CVE-2025-5419)

**Performance & stability**

*   Crash fixes across tabs, sidebar, startup, and settings

For a complete rundown, take a look at the [changelog](http://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-121/).

Happy browsing!
