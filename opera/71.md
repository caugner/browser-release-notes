Greetings everyone,

Today we are releasing the Opera 71 update. Among all the improvements, it includes some user-requested options for tab searching and history searching, as well as new customisable shortcut options for your messengers and workspaces. It’s all described in more detail below.

![](https://www-static-blogs.operacdn.com/desktop/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2020/09/O71__blog-post-image-hero-700x438.jpg)

### **Custom shortcuts for Messengers & Workspaces**

Messengers and workspaces have been given dedicated sections in Settings for customizing keyboard shortcuts. Now you can **create your own keyboard shortcut** for each messenger or workspace that you use, so you can open WhatsApp with Ctrl+M, for example, send a quick message, then jump to your shopping workspace with another keystroke. To create your own shortcuts, follow [these instructions](https://help.opera.com/en/latest/shortcuts/#keyboardShortcuts).

### **Search in tabs – Show more/less button**

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/x6uFK4bEVDYBM8DOx8hrKgUYo4HMlDHv8s8K3rbIxo9eyz1s26YnznGxxc2LbWbK60xYgIcXPa9ObqrqyAEis7wRRg_u3xPT442Sab2yAgsgq9h-PuRZQ0dift2U8FxFFZ-c8QY)

The **Search in Tabs** feature lets you search for specific tabs by typing keywords, showing you results from both your open tabs and your recently-closed tabs. Users have been requesting the option to see more of their tabs when searching, but we would also like to keep your search results clean and organized. 

To solve this, we’ve added a **Show more** button which will expand the results. This button changes to **Show less** after you expand it. This should give you the best of both worlds.

### **Advanced search options in History**

If you want to find a web page that you visited in the past, you can search through your records on the full History page (Ctrl+H) using keywords. However, what if you remember the date or time that you found the page, but can’t think of relevant keywords?

Thanks to more user requests, we’ve added **date filtering** to the search options. Now you can easily check where you were 2 weeks ago at 10:00. In addition, we’ve added a new **Delete History Below** button, which allows you to delete the history items you’ve found, without deleting the rest of your history.

See all the changes in [here](https://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-71/).

Happy browsing!
