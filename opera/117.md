Dear Opera Users!

We’re thrilled to announce the release of **stable version 117** (117.0.5408.32)!

One of the highlights of this release is the fix for the issue where “This tab was snoozed to save memory” would appear for no reason. We understand that this issue may have been frustrating for some, and we’re happy to have resolved it.

Additionally, we’ve updated Chromium to version **132.0.6834.83**, ensuring you get the latest features and security enhancements.

As always, we encourage you to share your thoughts with us – drop a comment below and let us know how the update is working for you!

Stay tuned for more updates, and thanks for being part of our community!

For a complete rundown, take a look at the [changelog](http://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-117/).

Happy browsing!
