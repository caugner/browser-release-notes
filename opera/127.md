Hello!

We’re happy to announce the release of **Opera 127 Stable**!

This release includes an update to **Chromium 143.0.7499.194**

### Key highlights

*   **We’ve resolved multiple crashes affecting areas such as:**
    *   browser settings and profile handling,
    *   split screen usage (including private windows and multi-tab layouts),
    *   onboarding flows when importing tabs from other browsers,
    *   Picture-in-Picture and sidebar miniplayer interactions.
*   **Tab Islands and tab management improvements:**
    *   tab island names are now shown in more places, including context menus and the Opera menu (on Windows and Linux),
    *   visual issues such as missing transparency and disappearing controls have been resolved.
*   **Enhanced Split Screen improvements (available in Early Bird)**
    *   incorrect layouts when duplicating or detaching split screens,
    *   blank or empty panes in certain configurations,
    *   problems with fullscreen mode hiding split screen layouts,
    *   crashes related to split screen creation and tab island interactions,
    *   improved menu wording and clearer exit options.

**Known issues:**

We’re aware of the following issues in this release and are already working on fixes:

*   **Free VPN:** In Opera 127 Stable, the Free VPN badge may not be displayed in the address bar. The VPN itself works correctly and can still be enabled and managed via **Settings**.
*   **Videoconferencing:** Some regressions affect videoconferencing tabs when they are detached, which may impact expected behavior during calls.
*   **Early Bird relaunch button visibility**: When opening the Settings through the Early Bird button from the Easy Setup section, the “Relaunch” button in the Early Bird section may not be visible.
*   **Enhanced Split Screen (**available**** **in Early Bird):** Pages don’t always adjust correctly to the split screen layout when navigating through links.

We’ll address these issues in upcoming updates. Thanks for your patience!

For a complete rundown, take a look at the [changelog](http://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-127/).

———

As always, thank you for your feedback! It helps us make Opera better with every release.

Happy browsing!
