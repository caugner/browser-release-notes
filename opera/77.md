Hello one and all!

![](https://www-static-blogs.operacdn.com/desktop/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/06/Opera-77-stable-release-700x394.jpg)

We are pleased to announce that Opera 77 has advanced to the stable version today. We’ve made a few tweaks and improvements, which are listed in [the changelog](https://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-77/#b4054.60), and we’re putting the final touches on some neat updates that will be available soon. Stay tuned!

Happy browsing!
