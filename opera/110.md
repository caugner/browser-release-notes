Hello!

We ale pleased to announce the release of new Opera stable update – **110.0.5130.23**!

Let’s look into key improvements:

*   **Chromium Update**: The browser has been updated to Chromium version **124.0.6367.62**, enhancing both performance and security.
*   **Scrollbar Button Fix**: The issue of invisible scrollbar buttons on Windows has been resolved, providing a smoother user experience.
*   **Enabled Features**:
    *   **Show Duplicate Indicator on Link**: This feature helps users identify and avoid navigating to duplicate links.
    *   **Drag Multiple Tabs**: Users can now drag multiple tabs simultaneously, improving tab management and workflow efficiency.

We value your feedback and invite you to share your thoughts on Opera’s latest update. Your comments and suggestions are crucial in helping us improve and refine our browser to better meet your needs.

EDIT: there is 0-day fix from Chromium included in this build. The changelog was updated.

Here’s the full [changelog](http://blogs.opera.com/desktop/changelog-for-110/).

Happy browsing!
